About Diya Jha

With an impressive track record of hosting 2,000+ events across corporate, experiential, lifestyle, automobile, education, and pharmaceutical sectors, Diya Jha is a household name in Indore and a trusted voice for national and international brands.

Bilingual in English and Hindi, Diya connects instantly with audiences, keeping energy levels high and events running smoothly. Her versatility spans annual conferences, dealer meets, product launches, government seminars, destination weddings, and large-scale concerts.
